Founder

Meet the founder

The person behind Cedars Abroad.

Ghina Awad

Founder, Cedars Abroad

Ghina Awad is an AI consultant, researcher, and instructor with over a decade of experience bridging artificial intelligence with finance, auditing, and management. She is a PhD candidate at the University of Montpellier (AI in interactive management control) and Universidad Complutense de Madrid (AI in internal audit), and has taught AI and finance courses at institutions including ESCE Business School, ESC Rennes School of Business, ICN Business School, and the University of Balamand. She has trained professionals on Ethics in Artificial Intelligence for the Institute of Internal Audit in both Jordan and Lebanon, serves on the editorial review board of the Journal of AI & Immersive Marketing, and reviews for several Emerald-published journals. Her book, AI Transformative Impact in Finance, Auditing, and Investment (IGI Global, Scopus-indexed), was published in 2025. Before moving into research and teaching, she spent over a decade in banking as a Senior Operations Officer at Bank of Beirut. Cedars Abroad brings that same practical, research-backed approach to AI training, certification, and hands-on implementation.
